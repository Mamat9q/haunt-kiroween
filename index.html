HAUNT

You wake in a haunted manor. Collect the 3 relics, avoid the Warden, and escape before your sanity runs out. The Warden narrates your every step.

Difficulty

WASD / arrows — move  ·  E / Space — interact (relic, riddle door)
L — lantern on/off (saves oil; darkness drains sanity)  ·  M — mute
P / Esc — pause  ·  1 / 2 / 3 — answer a riddle
Candle-lit rooms restore sanity. Locked doors need keys. Riddle doors need the right answer.

Fully playable with no setup — the offline Warden narrates by default. Add your own free OpenRouter key in Settings for AI narration. Your key never leaves your browser.
